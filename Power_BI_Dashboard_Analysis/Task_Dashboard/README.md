📢 TechCorp Project & Workforce Dashboard 📊 Project Overview The TechCorp Project & Workforce Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze company-wide project delivery, task execution, and employee performance. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into project budgets, delivery timelines, task status, and workforce performance, enabling managers and leadership to make informed, data-driven decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

Monitor project portfolio health and delivery status.
Track key project management KPIs.
Analyze task progress, blockers, and delivery timelines.
Evaluate milestone achievement and schedule risk.
Assess employee performance and workload distribution.
Support resourcing and delivery decisions through interactive reporting.

💡 Key Insights

Only 1 of 15 projects is marked Completed, while 7 remain On Hold and 2 are Cancelled, leaving average completion at just 49%.
Blocked tasks (43) form the largest task-status group, ahead of In Progress and In Review, pointing to delivery bottlenecks.
Milestones show real schedule risk — 19 Delayed versus only 16 Achieved out of 65 total.
Two projects are running over their approved budget: Marketing Campaign Program C (+7.9%) and Security Audit Program A (+5.1%), the latter despite being only 17% complete.
14 employees are allocated over 100% capacity across active projects, a likely contributor to blocked tasks and delayed milestones.
Average performance score sits at 3.65/5, with 16 of 60 reviews recommending promotion.

🚀 Business Recommendations

Prioritize unblocking the 43 Blocked tasks before starting new work.
Re-baseline or formally close out the 7 On-Hold projects.
Audit the 2 over-budget projects before costs run further ahead of progress.
Rebalance workload for over-allocated employees, starting with those above 200% allocation.
Provide coaching or development plans for employees scoring below 3.0.

These recommendations are based on the dashboard analysis and are intended to improve delivery predictability and workforce utilization.
